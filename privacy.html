Privacy Policy

Etsy Automation · operated by The Wandering Yank · Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. About this application

Etsy Automation (the “App”) is a private, internal workflow tool operated by The Wandering Yank (thewanderingyank.com). It is used only by its owner to run the owner’s own print-on-demand Etsy shop: it helps plan weekly product designs, prepare product drafts and store product images. The App is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Google user data we access

The App connects to the owner’s own Google account through Google OAuth and uses Google Drive only. Within a single designated Drive folder it can:

  • create weekly sub-folders;
  • upload AI-generated design images and product mockup images;
  • find and download the design file that the owner has prepared for a given day.

The App does not read your email, contacts, calendar or any Drive content unrelated to this folder.

3. How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the functions described above. Design artwork the owner places in the folder is sent to the print-on-demand provider (Printify) and to AI image services, solely to create products and mockups for the owner’s shop. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not share it with any other third party.

4. Storage and security

The App runs on a self-hosted automation server controlled by the owner. OAuth credentials are stored encrypted on that server and are used only to call Google APIs on the owner’s behalf. We keep Drive content in the owner’s own Google Drive; we do not maintain a separate copy of Google user data.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. No human reads Google user data except the owner, and only where needed for security, to comply with law, or to operate the App.

6. Retention and deletion

Files in Google Drive stay there until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and by deleting the Google credential in the automation server. Revoking access stops the App from reading or writing any Google data.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: please use the contact information at thewanderingyank.com.
